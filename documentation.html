Introduction

The Lung Cancer Transcription Factor Database (Lung TFDB) is a specialized, comprehensive resource designed for researchers studying the role of transcription factors (TFs) in the pathology of lung cancer. It provides a centralized, user-friendly platform for accessing and analyzing a wide array of multi-omics data related to TFs. This includes their protein sequences, somatic mutations, genomic binding sites, gene expression levels, and their impact on patient survival outcomes. Our goal is to accelerate research by providing pre-compiled and easy-to-explore datasets.

Features

Protein Sequence

View, search, and download the full-length protein sequences of specified transcription factors. This feature is useful for local sequence analysis, such as domain prediction or alignment.

Mutations

Explore somatic mutations in TFs across different lung cancer subtypes (LUAD, LUSC). The data is visualized using an interactive OncoPlot, which displays the type and frequency of mutations for multiple genes across a patient cohort. This helps in identifying frequently mutated TFs that may be key drivers in lung tumorigenesis.

Binding Sites

Identify and visualize the genomic binding sites of TFs. This data is critical for understanding the regulatory networks controlled by these factors and how they influence gene expression.

scRNA-seq Analysis

Analyze single-cell RNA sequencing data to understand TF expression at the cellular level. This section provides UMAP plots for visualizing cell clusters, feature plots to see the expression of a gene within those clusters, and violin plots to compare expression across different cell types.

Expression Analysis

Compare TF expression levels between lung tumor tissues and adjacent normal tissues. This differential expression analysis is visualized using box plots and violin plots, providing a clear view of TFs that are up-regulated or down-regulated in cancer.

Survival Analysis

Perform Kaplan-Meier survival analysis to assess the prognostic significance of a TF's expression. This tool allows researchers to determine if high or low expression of a TF is correlated with patient survival outcomes, helping to identify potential prognostic biomarkers.

Data Sources

The data within Lung TFDB is meticulously compiled and curated from several publicly available, high-quality repositories. We ensure data consistency and quality through a standardized processing pipeline. Key sources include:

  • The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA): Specifically, the TCGA-LUAD (Lung Adenocarcinoma) and TCGA-LUSC (Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma) cohorts for mutation, expression, and clinical data.
  • Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO): For independent gene expression datasets and single-cell RNA-seq experiments.
  • cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics: As an additional source for cross-validation of mutation and expression data.
  • Other peer-reviewed scientific publications containing relevant lung cancer datasets.

Each dataset is harmonized to ensure gene identifiers and sample annotations are consistent across the database.

Technical Details

Lung TFDB is built with a standard web stack. The frontend is created with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The backend data processing and analysis tasks are handled by PHP scripts that execute R code for statistical computations and plot generation. The interactive visualizations are powered by various JavaScript libraries.
