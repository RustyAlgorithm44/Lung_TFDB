Introduction

The Lung Cancer Transcription Factor Database (Lung TFDB) is a comprehensive resource for researchers studying the role of transcription factors (TFs) in lung cancer. It provides a centralized platform for accessing and analyzing a wide range of data related to TFs, including their protein sequences, mutations, binding sites, expression levels, and impact on patient survival.

Features

  • Protein Sequence: View and download the protein sequences of TFs.
  • Mutations: Explore somatic mutations in TFs across different lung cancer subtypes.
  • Binding Sites: Identify and visualize the genomic binding sites of TFs.
  • scRNA-seq: Analyze single-cell RNA sequencing data to understand TF expression at the cellular level.
  • Expression: Compare TF expression levels between tumor and normal tissues.
  • Survival: Perform survival analysis to assess the prognostic significance of TFs.
  • Download: Download various datasets for offline analysis.
  • Contact: Get in touch with the database administrators for support or inquiries.

Data Sources

The data in Lung TFDB is compiled from various public sources, including but not limited to:

  • The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA)
  • Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)
  • cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics
  • And other relevant scientific publications.

Each dataset is carefully curated and processed to ensure data quality and consistency.
